Frontend · chosen palette

Baltic: warm greys, kelp purple

Option C's warm greys stay exactly as they were: same page, surface, text and border colors. The accent is now a muted kelp purple, the deep plum of the red and purple seaweeds along the Baltic shore. It's earthy rather than the bright violet of AI products, and it stays clear of the warning and misleading colors.

Rules that keep it working

Purple, kept apart from its neighbours

The accent is darker and greyer than the chart violet and pink, and far from the "False" red, so a button never looks like a data point or a verdict. Keep the accent out of charts; charts use their own colors below.
